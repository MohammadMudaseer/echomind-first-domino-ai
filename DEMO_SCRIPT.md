# Three-minute demo script

**0:00–0:25 — Problem**  
“Urban failures are connected. A blocked drain during intense rainfall may cause waterlogging, road disruption, traffic congestion, and delayed emergency access. Most simple dashboards show isolated indicators; EchoMind focuses on the chain.”

**0:25–0:55 — Scenario**  
“This is a fictional neighborhood. I set rainfall to 75 mm, blockage to 85%, and starting traffic load to 65%. These are scenario inputs, not live measurements.”

**0:55–1:30 — First Domino**  
“The graph shows the dependency chain. The current First Domino candidate is selected by a transparent heuristic that considers the starting hazard and the risk of downstream nodes. It is a prioritization hint, not a causal proof.”

**1:30–2:15 — Intervention**  
“I select ‘Clear the critical drain’. The baseline and intervention columns update. This is a counterfactual inside our simulated rules: it demonstrates how the product could compare actions, not a measured reduction in real flood risk.”

**2:15–2:40 — Other options**  
“We can also test pumps, road protection, or early traffic diversion. Different interventions affect different parts of the chain.”

**2:40–3:00 — Honesty and roadmap**  
“This MVP is rule-based, not trained ML. Next we would collect reliable, timestamped local data, validate against simple baselines, calibrate uncertainty, and work with city experts before making real-world predictions.”
